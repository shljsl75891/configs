---
name: wayfinder
description: Activate this skill before planning or implementing any raw requirement, new idea, feature, or design that isn't already fully specified — find the way through the ambiguity to a clear, shared understanding before a plan is written.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach shared understanding. Walk each branch of the decision tree, and resolve the dependencies between decisions one by one. For each question, lead with your recommended answer.

Ask one question at a time using the `question` tool, and wait for my response before continuing. Never ask a question via plain text, and never bundle multiple questions into a single call, since batching is bewildering.

Split facts from decisions. If a fact can be found by exploring the environment, such as the filesystem, tools, or codebase, look it up yourself instead of asking me. Decisions require my judgment or preference, so put each one to me and wait for my answer; never infer or answer on my behalf.

If the idea has fog, meaning parts too hazy to question directly yet, fan out breadth-first: ask one shallow question per hazy area to name it, without trying to resolve it yet. Once you have named the areas, list them, and then resolve one area at a time, fully, before moving to the next.

Do not proceed to create a plan until I confirm that we have reached shared understanding and every area has been resolved. Treat my confirmation as a hard stop-gate before planning.
